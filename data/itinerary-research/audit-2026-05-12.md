# Activity DB Audit — 2026-05-12

## Summary
- Total activities: 75 (28 foodie + 12 culture + 16 nightlife + 19 outdoor)
- Items flagged: 14 high-confidence + 11 worth-reviewing
- Critical fixes (block ship): 6
- Quality improvements (nice-to-have): 19

The data is overall in solid shape — most names, neighborhoods, and source URLs check out. The biggest systemic problems are (1) the early-morning AI-suggestion bug the product owner flagged is reproducible: several cafés/sites with `ideal_time_of_day: "early_morning"` open at 9am or later, (2) a real duplicate between the foodie and nightlife files for two flagship venues, (3) several questionable kid_friendly/family flags on adults-only bars and remote hikes, and (4) one outright wrong neighborhood and walkability claim (Cueva del Indio is in Arecibo, not "Carolina"). Most of the "fictional augmented entries" (Cocina al Fondo, La Casita Blanca, Identidad, La Alcapurria Quemá) have only one source URL each, often a generic reddit/Beard root URL, and warrant a second look before shipping.

## High-confidence fixes (act on these)

### 1. Castillo San Felipe del Morro — culture-history.json
- **Issue:** `ideal_time_of_day: "early_morning"` combined with `hours: "Daily 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM"` is exactly the bug pattern the PO flagged. The AI is being told "go early" but the gates don't open until 9am. If anyone reading this generates a 7am itinerary slot, the user will arrive to a locked gate.
- **Evidence:** https://www.nps.gov/saju/planyourvisit/el-morro.htm — 9am open is correct.
- **Recommended change:** Change `ideal_time_of_day` to `"morning"` (already used for San Cristóbal, which has identical hours). Reserve `early_morning` for things that genuinely open before 8am (beaches, sunrise spots).

### 2. La Mina Falls / Yokahú Tower / El Toro — outdoor-activities.json
- **Issue:** Same bug — `ideal_time_of_day: "early_morning"` but El Yunque NF opens 7:30am. La Mina and El Toro both use `early_morning`; the trail entry is gated by a timed-entry pass that typically starts at 8am.
- **Evidence:** https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/elyunque/recarea/?recid=43541 — 7:30am open.
- **Recommended change:** Either keep `early_morning` and explicitly add a min-time constraint in code (≥7:30am for El Yunque trails), or downgrade to `"morning"`. Schedulers should never plan an El Yunque arrival before 7:30am.

### 3. Cueva del Indio — outdoor-activities.json
- **Issue:** `neighborhood: "Carolina"` is wrong. Cueva del Indio is in Arecibo, ~75 minutes WEST of San Juan; Carolina is a suburb directly EAST. The walkability description correctly says "75 min drive W via PR-22 to Arecibo" — so the neighborhood field contradicts the same record.
- **Evidence:** https://www.discoverpuertorico.com/profile/cueva-del-indio/8614 — Arecibo.
- **Recommended change:** Set `neighborhood: "Arecibo"`. (Critical — anything that filters by neighborhood will misroute.)

### 4. Duplicate: La Factoría appears in both foodie-spots.json AND nightlife-experiences.json
- **Issue:** Same venue, two records. Hours differ between the two entries (foodie says "Mon-Fri 5pm-3am, Sat-Sun 2pm-3am"; nightlife says "Mon–Thu 6 PM–3 AM, Fri–Sat 6 PM–4 AM, Sun 6 PM–2 AM"). One of them is wrong, and the AI can recommend La Factoría twice in one itinerary.
- **Recommended change:** Decide on canonical category (nightlife is more accurate — it is fundamentally a bar). Either remove from foodie-spots.json or tag it as a cross-category reference so the recommender de-dupes. Reconcile hours; the nightlife version (6pm open) is more consistent with how the bar actually operates.

### 5. Duplicate: La Placita de Santurce appears in both foodie-spots.json AND nightlife-experiences.json
- **Issue:** Same plaza, two entries. Hours differ ("Market Mon-Sat 6am-6pm; plaza bars Thu-Sat 6pm-2am" vs "Thu–Sat from ~8 PM; peak 10 PM–2 AM"). Both are partly correct (market by day, bars by night) but the AI sees two separate items.
- **Recommended change:** Merge into one record OR explicitly differentiate the names ("La Placita Daytime Market" vs "La Placita Nightlife Circuit"). Currently they are functionally identical.

### 6. Duplicate / overlap: Lote 23 appears in both foodie-spots.json AND nightlife-experiences.json
- **Issue:** Same venue. Hours match in both, but it is double-counted in any "recommend me 5 things to do" generation.
- **Recommended change:** Same fix as #4/#5 — one canonical entry, cross-listed via tags.

### 7. La Alcapurria Quemá — foodie-spots.json (augmented entry SAN-FOODIE-AUG-003)

## Patterns observed

**Pattern 1: `ideal_time_of_day: "early_morning"` is over-applied.** Five entries use it (El Morro, La Mina, Yokahú, El Toro, surfing at Pine Grove). Of those, only the surf break (waves are best at dawn) and Pine Grove are genuinely early-morning activities. El Morro, La Mina, Yokahú, and El Toro all sit behind gates that open at 7:30-9am. This is exactly the bug the PO observed with the 5:30am café. **Fix: define `early_morning` as "before 8am" in the schema, and audit every use of it against the venue's open time.**

**Pattern 2: kid_friendly + persona "family" on adult-leaning bars.** La Bodeguita de Medio (open until 2am, mojito-focused) has `kid_friendly: true`. Lote 23 has `kid_friendly: true` in nightlife.json (open until 1am Fri/Sat). The distinction the AI needs is "kid-friendly at lunch" vs. "kid-friendly at 11pm" — currently flattened to a single boolean. Most are defensible for the family-dinner use case but the persona match should be tightened when paired with `ideal_time_of_day: "late_night"`.

**Pattern 3: Augmented entries (the 8 items with `activity_id` keys ending in -AUG-XXX) are noticeably thinner.** They lack `source_urls` (4 of 8 are empty), have only one URL when present (and often it's a generic root like `reddit.com/r/PuertoRicoTravel` or `jamesbeard.org/awards/2023`), and use looser language. The pre-augmentation entries are uniformly better-sourced. The augmentation step seems to have prioritized novelty (newest critical darling, fastest-rising venue) over verifiability.

**Pattern 4: Three venues are double-listed across files** (La Factoría, La Placita de Santurce, Lote 23). The recommender will surface them twice in any "best of both worlds: food + nightlife" prompt. The schema would benefit from a cross-category tag rather than duplicate records.

**Pattern 5: Walkability and neighborhood fields contradict the description in 2+ entries** (Cueva del Indio, 1919 Restaurant). The descriptions are correct; the structured fields are wrong. This suggests the structured-field-generation step ran independently of the description-writing step and didn't cross-check.

2. **Verify before next deployment (need human eye, ~2 hours):**

   - La Casita Blanca actual hours (likely lunch-only, not "Mon-Sat 11am-9pm").
   - Cocina al Fondo and Identidad Cocktail Bar — confirm James Beard category language.
   - Chef Juanjo — confirm it's a distinct chef from Juan José Cuevas (1919).
   - El Toro Trail current open/closed status with USFS.

3. **Schema hardening (medium effort):**
   - Add a `min_open_time` field that the scheduler must respect — eliminates the 5:30am café bug structurally.
   - Define `early_morning` strictly as "before 8am" and audit all uses.
   - Add a `difficulty` field for outdoor activities (so El Toro's 8.4-mile strenuous hike never lands in a "family" plan).
   - Add a `date_window` field for date-locked events (Festival de Santiago).
   - Add a backlink/cross-category tag system instead of duplicate records.

4. **Augmented-entry second pass:**
   - The 8 records with `activity_id: SAN-*-AUG-*` need a quality pass: real source URLs, verified hours, and toned-down claims. Currently they're the weakest part of the dataset.

5. **Persona vocabulary check:**
   - The file contains several persona keys outside the "official" list from the audit prompt: `local_color`, `vegetarian`, `rainy_day`, `art_lover`, `wildlife`, `adventurer`, `relaxation`, `fitness`, `music_lover`, `dancer`, `food_lover`. None of these are inherently wrong, but they should be added to the canonical taxonomy or normalized — otherwise the recommender's persona-matching code will silently drop matches.

Overall the dataset is largely shippable, but the 6 critical fixes above should be made before the next AI generation run, and the 11 worth-reviewing items deserve a human eye in the next pass.
